✨ Meet the Creator

Hi, I'm Hari Prasath M.

Author · Creator · App Developer

Welcome to my creative world! I'm Hari Prasath M., the creator of Kids Park, a fun and colorful website made to help children enjoy stories, imagination, and learning.

I'm also an independent author. Some of my children's books are published through Amazon Kindle Direct Publishing (KDP). I have also published a Tamil motivational paperback book through Pothi.

I created the Grow App, a quiz app published on the Google Play Store. Through my websites, books, apps, and motivational content, I aim to make learning enjoyable and encourage people to keep growing.
