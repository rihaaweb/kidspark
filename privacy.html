🔒 Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us.

🐣 Introduction

Welcome to Kids Park. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you visit and use the Kids Park website.

Kids Park is designed to provide children and families with simple educational content, stories and learning activities.

Important: We aim to collect only information that is necessary for operating and improving the website.

📋 Information We Collect

Kids Park may not require visitors to create an account or provide personal information simply to browse and use the educational content.

Depending on future website features, we may receive information that visitors voluntarily provide, such as information submitted through a contact form.

We may also receive limited technical information automatically through hosting, analytics, advertising or security services.

🍪 Cookies

Kids Park may use cookies and similar technologies to help the website function properly and to understand how visitors use the website.

Cookies may be used by third-party services that are integrated into the website in the future.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Some website features may not work correctly if certain cookies are disabled.

📢 Advertising

Kids Park may display advertisements in the future. We may use third-party advertising services such as Google AdSense to display advertisements on the website.

These advertising services may use cookies or similar technologies to provide, measure and personalize advertisements, subject to the applicable settings and policies of the advertising provider.

Future advertising: Advertising may not be active on all pages or at the time this Privacy Policy is published. This section describes possible future advertising features.

When advertising is enabled, visitors should review the applicable privacy and advertising information provided by the advertising provider.

👶 Children's Privacy

Kids Park is intended to provide child-friendly educational and entertainment content.

We do not intentionally request children to provide unnecessary personal information simply to access ordinary learning content.

Parents and guardians are encouraged to supervise children's use of websites and online services, particularly when third-party services, advertising or external links are available.

🔗 Third-Party Services

Kids Park may use third-party services to provide certain website functions, such as hosting, analytics, advertising, payment processing or other services.

These third-party providers may have their own privacy policies and terms. We encourage users and parents or guardians to review the policies of third-party services when appropriate.

📚 Ebooks and Digital Products

In the future, Kids Park may offer our own ebooks, educational resources or other digital products for purchase.

If digital products are offered, we may use a third-party payment provider to process payments. Payment information may be handled directly by the payment provider according to its privacy policy and terms.

Kids Park does not need to store complete payment card details when payment processing is handled by an appropriate third-party payment provider.

Product pages will provide additional information about the applicable purchase terms, delivery method, refunds or other conditions when products become available.

🛡️ Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled through the website.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information online can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

🌐 External Links

Kids Park may contain links to external websites, services or resources.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content or security of external websites. Users should review the privacy policies of external websites before providing personal information.

🔄 Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time when new features, services or legal requirements are introduced.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

📅 Effective Date

This Privacy Policy is effective from September 23, 2026.

📩 Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, you can contact us through the Kids Park Contact Us page.
